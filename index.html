Opening Minds Develops the World

Opentra is an Educational Technology Company that develops digital tools to catalyse learning and development. Moved by the befief that education is the most powerful weapon, and that technology is not the modern day's enemy, but a tool to be used to facilitate the human evolution, Opentra provides platforms and materials to make the learning and teaching process more accessible and efficient.
